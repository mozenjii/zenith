Section 03 // Process

HOW I
WORK.

01
First Principles Research

Every project begins by stripping away assumptions. I spend 40% of the timeline in the "Abstract Layer," mapping out data flows and constraints before touching a text editor.

02
Rapid Prototyping Loops

Speed is a feature. I build functional, high-fidelity prototypes early to test structural integrity under load. Failure is optimized to happen in the first week.

03
Obsessive Refinement

The final 20% of effort produces 80% of the polish. I fine-tune latency, accessibility, and micro-interactions until the interface feels physical.
